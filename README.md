# COG Testimonies

A standalone testimony platform for Chapel of Grace, created to give people a place to share what God has done in their lives and encourage others through their stories.

## Overview

COG Testimonies is a church-focused website designed around storytelling, faith, and community.

Visitors can explore testimonies, submit their own stories, and connect with the church through its social media and location information.

## Features

- Responsive homepage
- Testimony submission form
- Testimony categories
- Testimony collection
- Individual testimony reading experience
- Photo and video upload support
- Publication consent options
- Form submission notifications
- Church social media links
- Church location and service information
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Smooth animations and transitions

## Form Submission

The testimony form is connected to Forminit.

When a visitor submits a testimony:

1. The form collects the visitor's information.
2. The submission is sent to Forminit.
3. The church receives the submission through its configured email.
4. The church can review the testimony before publishing it.
